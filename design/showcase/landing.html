How it works

One lesson, four simple steps.

Every lesson follows the same calm pattern, so children always know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Look and listen

    Picture flashcards, one short sentence each. The voice reads it and lights up each word.

  2. 2

    Play a game

    The game uses the same words. A wrong tap gets gentle help, never a buzzer.

  3. 3

    Earn a reward

    Stars for right answers on the first try, then a medal or a trophy at the end.

  4. From your child’s mentorA note about today’s play, written for you.
    4

    A mentor follows

    The mentor sees every play and writes notes the family can read.

Questions

Good to know.

Who is Speechomania for?

Autistic children working on speech and language, with their families, their mentors and the centre that supports them.

How do we get an account?

Accounts come from your centre. The centre enrols your child, gives them a mentor and chooses their lessons. Then you sign in and your child’s path is ready.

How long is one lesson?

Every lesson has at least 15 activities: flashcards first, then a game with the same words. Most children finish one in a short sitting.

What does the mentor do?

The mentor sees each game your child plays, writes notes for you, and can suggest changes to lessons or give an assessment.

Who can see my child’s progress?

Only your family, your child’s mentor and your centre. Mentors only see the children they look after.

Does it need special equipment?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or laptop. Sound on helps, because every word is read aloud.
