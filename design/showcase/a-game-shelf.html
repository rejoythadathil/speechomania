How a lesson works

1

Look and listen

Part 1 is flashcards. Each card shows one picture and one short sentence, read aloud word by word.

2

Play the game

Part 2 opens after the cards. The child uses the same words in a game, with gentle help after a wrong try.

3

A mentor follows along

Every child has a mentor who sees each play and writes notes for the family.
