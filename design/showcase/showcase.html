How it works

Two parts and a reward, every time.

The same calm pattern in every lesson, so a child always knows what comes next.

Part 1

Flashcards

One picture and one short sentence on each card, read aloud word by word.

Part 2

A game with the same words

A wrong tap gets gentle help and another try.

The end

A reward that is earned

    A new friend every stars

    Who it is for

    Everyone around the child, on one platform.

    Families

    • The child’s learning path opens as soon as you sign in.
    • A check-in every 15 minutes, and a rest after an hour.
    • Notes from the mentor.

    Mentors

    • Every game each child plays: right answers, tries and time.
    • Notes for the family, or for the team.
    • Assessments and lesson suggestions.

    Centres

    • Enrol children and give each one a mentor.
    • Choose the lessons each child gets.
    • Build assessments and preview any game.
