# Ready-to-use sprite prompts

Copy one prompt block into the image generator as it is. Each block already includes the style, so all sheets match. Generate in this order: O and P first, then B, I and A. L, M and N are optional.

Save each sheet as `images-generated/new-spirit/sheet-<letter>.png` (for example `sheet-o.png`). The names under each prompt are what each picture becomes, in grid order; they are for slicing, not for the generator.

## Sheet O: Girl actions
Needed now: unlocks the He and she and They lessons.

```text
A sprite sheet of 12 items. Each is the same girl (warm brown skin, black hair in two plaits, yellow dress), full body, doing the action: 1) reading a book, 2) eating rice from a plate with a spoon, 3) jumping with both feet off the ground, 4) waving one hand, 5) sleeping in a bed with a blanket, 6) painting at an easel, 7) dancing, 8) writing with a pencil at a desk, 9) singing with music notes, 10) cooking at a small pot, 11) riding a bicycle, 12) swimming in a small patch of blue water.

Cute 2D cartoon sticker illustrations for a calm learning app for young autistic children, matching one consistent set: soft rounded shapes, a clean dark-brown outline of even thickness, bright but gentle flat colours with one soft shade, simple friendly faces with round eyes, no scary or realistic details. Arrange the items in a grid of 4 columns and 3 rows, in exactly the order listed (left to right, then top to bottom), each item centred in its cell with lots of empty space around it. Items must not touch or overlap, and each item is one single connected object. Plain pure white background (#FFFFFF), no ground shadows, no frames, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark. People are friendly children or adults with warm brown skin and black hair.
```

Names in order: girl-reading, girl-eating, girl-jumping, girl-waving, girl-sleeping, girl-painting, girl-dancing, girl-writing, girl-singing, girl-cooking, girl-cycling, girl-swimming
